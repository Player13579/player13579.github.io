## Numerical mechanism and its exact scope

The camera ray is p=(screenX-sourceX,screenY+.36*depth,depth). Near/far intervals are clipped against y=0 and y=1.55 before partitioning, so a cell does not span outside the physical field. The selected32 or64 intervals use the same finite near/far domain. All ray positions and half intervals are in the shader's declared world/extent coordinates.

Inside one cell, the normalized anisotropic gas coordinates r are linearized along the ray using analytic center and width derivatives. The reacting-sheet distance s is linearized with center, rotating-normal, offset and thickness derivatives. A sum of squared affine Gaussian coordinates is A*t^2+2*B*t+C. Its average over [-h,h] is calculated by completing the square and an erf difference: exp(-(C-B^2/A)) times the Gaussian interval average at center B/A and sigma1/sqrt(A). The residual clamp to0 only handles Cauchy roundoff; these coefficients are sums of squares. A zero derivative uses its exact constant-field limit. For a normalized half interval below.125, four-node symmetric Gauss-Legendre quadrature evaluates the local Gaussian directly, avoiding cancellation of almost equal erf values. Larger cells use the erf approximation. The independent CPU worker found an initial near-constant-cell value1.0008925995 where direct integration gives approximately1; this defect was repaired before source sealing, and its failure evidence is retained separately. erf uses a documented polynomial approximation. Tiny Gaussian tails can lose precision through erf subtraction, and f32 implementation is not inferred from CPU double results.

This integrates the local quadratic model using analytic erf intervals and stable quadrature for near-constant cells. It does NOT exactly integrate the nonlinear center/width rotation over the cell, moving cooling envelope, supply, radial ignition, or ordered attenuation/emission interaction. Those outer factors are evaluated at the cell midpoint. Source thickness has a finite physical scale, and the projected horizontal Gaussian includes pixel variance p^2/6. A width-preserving amplitude factor retains integrated horizontal mass after widening; core and reaction still require native image comparison. This is approximate physical pixel support, not a claim of perfect multidimensional pixel integration.

Absorption is extinction=1.8*density. Cell transfer uses (1-exp(-extinction*length))/extinction, with its length limit for near-zero extinction. Reaction/core emissivity is independent of density and integrated with this transfer. Separate density, reaction, hot core and heat are returned; medium alpha remains actual source transmittance. The observer receives the same radiance/medium attachments as R4. The runtime uniform ABI remains80bytes/20floats with depthSamples at index16 and three zero padding values; the first16 values and two passes are unchanged.

Default depth remains32; diagnostic64 is preserved. R4's serial submit-to-complete examples were not a GPU throughput measurement and are not a reason alone to set64. R5's new local analytic coverage is tested independently on CPU, but root must compare matched source/cause/generation/viewport at several ages32/64, main-only and normal OBS, and capture a natural default32 lifetime.
