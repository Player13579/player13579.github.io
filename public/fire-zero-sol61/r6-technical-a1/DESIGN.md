## R6 visual change
R5's actual normal H64 public replay showed red-orange floor receiving light below the active flames ending in straight rectangular top and side edges. Playback age was not recorded. R6 replaces the three-height receiver estimate with a continuous ground-plane calculation from the same three feeds sampled at the same three supplied heights. Nine contributions follow existing fuel, ignition, transported position and cooling support, then attenuate with receiver distance and floor incidence. This remains an approximation, not integrated radiometry.

The full volumetric flame shape and material, three feed geometry, supply/ignition/cooling clock, absorption/emission, PH/OBS two-pass structure, receiver controls, actor light, heat/glare and bright-background behavior remain as R5. This preserves ignition/rising, sustained flame, cutoff, cooling and convergence. The source does not prove that the observed rectangular floor patch originates in the receiver equation; R5's equation itself has continuous spatial falloff. Therefore this is a narrow floor-receiver improvement candidate and does not claim the hard-edge defect is repaired. Actual comparison must reject it if the edge remains in radiance/source.
