## Real improvement versus unresolved material

The rear/body contribution is now visibly broad rather than only a thin upper feather. The white hot path is visibly displaced to the lower edge; these are actual consequences of the R16 projection change. The shape is attached to the source, has soft exterior edges and a finite tapered front. The old solid casing/double rim/orange arrow is absent. The main colour support is no longer equivalent to R15's faint nonzero margins.

The unresolved appearance is therefore not simply a repeat of the thinness diagnosis. The broad body looks like a nearly continuous blue strip. Its internal variation is mild and predominantly longitudinal. The lower white path remains read as a luminous edging, including around the shallow central depression. That depression changes the profile but does not clearly show a nearer charge crossing and concealing a hot interior behind it. The resulting blue/white relationship gives width, but weak spatial/material depth. A reader can reasonably identify a clean luminous beam, yet the selected thick charge-body relationship is not convincingly realised.

This is a concrete initial-gate deficiency against R16's chosen expression contract. It is not a universal requirement that all beams expose a rear opening or that every plasma has opaque faces. Strong white is permitted: neither white brightness nor clipping is the fail criterion. A valid different charge/plasma approach could use other depth/transport cues; it must satisfy the subject rather than merely relabel the current band. Conversely, granting volume quality because the blue region is now wider would repeat the numerical-support/perceived-material substitution that the R16 diagnosis explicitly warned against.

Whether ordinary movement would strengthen the charge interpretation is **unobserved** here. This report neither grants that improvement nor asserts a transport failure. The three held images are sufficient to reject the selected initial near/hot-path material relationship; they are insufficient to reject or accept continuous advection, timing and the full artistic event.
