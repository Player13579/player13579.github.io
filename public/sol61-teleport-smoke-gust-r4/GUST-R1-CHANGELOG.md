# Gust R1

Derived from sealed white-smoke R4. Replaced the small periodic x wobble with continuous, positive, decaying gust advection and height shear. Added smooth differential curl/roll and current CPU-derived support to WGSL's bounds and depth rays. Changed ray sample count to sixteen and scale extinction with ray-step length. Preserved source pixels, SFX, actor motion, receipt and E-clock scheduling. Native replay and quality acceptance are pending.
